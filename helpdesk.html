Frequently Asked Questions

To request academic documents, log in to your portal account, go to "New Request", choose your desired document type from the options provided, complete the necessary details, and set your preferred appointment schedule for verification or pickup.
You can manage your existing appointments from your Student Dashboard under "My Appointments". Requests for rescheduling or cancellation must be submitted at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled time slot.
On the Login page, click on "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email address. You will receive an email containing instructions on how to reset your password.
Please present a valid ID along with your appointment confirmation slip (digital or printed). If sending a representative, they must present an Authorization Letter, a copy of your valid ID, and their own valid ID.
